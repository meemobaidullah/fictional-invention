FAQ

Questions sellers ask before they hire us

What does a full-service Amazon agency actually do?

We take ownership of the work that moves an Amazon brand: account setup and health, listing and keyword optimization, PPC across Sponsored Products, Brands and Display, A+ content and Storefront design, inventory planning, and expansion into new marketplaces. You get one team and one plan instead of five freelancers.

Do you work with new sellers or only established brands?

Both. New sellers get a launch plan built on market and competitor research. Established brands start with an audit of their account, catalog and ad spend so we fix the biggest leaks first.

How quickly will I see results?

Quick wins such as fixing suppressed listings, cutting wasted ad spend and repairing keyword gaps usually show within the first 30 days. Compounding gains in organic rank and profitability typically build over 60 to 90 days.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. We work month to month after an initial onboarding period, because we would rather keep clients with results than with paperwork.

Which Amazon marketplaces do you support?

We manage brands on Amazon US, Canada, UK, the EU marketplaces, Australia and the UAE, and we can plan your expansion from one to the others.

What is included in the free account audit?

A review of your listings, keyword coverage, PPC structure and spend, account health and pricing, delivered as a short report with your top priorities ranked by revenue impact.
