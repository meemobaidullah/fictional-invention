Reinstatement Questions

01How long does reinstatement take?

Simple listing appeals can be resolved in days. Account suspensions usually take 1 to 4 weeks, depending on the cause and Amazon's response.

02Can you guarantee reinstatement?

No honest agency can guarantee Amazon's decision. We do guarantee a thorough root-cause review and a well-written plan of action.

03Should I appeal myself first?

Ideally not. Weak appeals can make later ones harder. Contact us before you submit.

04Do you handle FBA reimbursements?

Yes. We audit lost, damaged and miscounted inventory and file claims for what Amazon owes you.

05Do you work on related-account suspensions?

Yes. These need careful evidence, and we will tell you honestly what your chances are.
