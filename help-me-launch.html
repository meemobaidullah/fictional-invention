Launch Questions, Answered

01How long does it take to launch on Amazon?

Most launches take 6 to 10 weeks from kickoff to the product going live, depending on account verification, Brand Registry, photography and how quickly inventory reaches the fulfillment center.

02Do I need a trademark before I launch?

You can sell without one, but Brand Registry needs a registered or pending trademark, and it unlocks A+ content, Storefronts, Sponsored Brands and brand protection. We help you plan the timing so the trademark doesn't hold up your launch.

03Can you help me choose which product to launch?

Yes. Our research looks at demand, competition, pricing, fees and margin for your product ideas, so you start with the one that has the best chance of turning a profit.

04Should I use FBA or ship orders myself?

For most new brands FBA is the faster way to win Prime buyers, but it depends on your product size, margins and volumes. We compare FBA and merchant fulfillment costs for your products and recommend one.

05How do you get the first reviews?

We use Amazon-approved programs such as Amazon Vine and the Request a Review button, plus strong listings and launch ads that drive real sales. We never use paid or incentivized reviews, which break Amazon's rules.

06What happens after the launch?

After launch we keep optimizing ads, keywords and pricing for the first 90 days. You can then move to a monthly management plan or take over with the playbook we hand you.
