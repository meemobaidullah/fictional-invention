Signs Your Brand Is Losing Sales on Amazon

Inconsistent look

Every listing looks different

Weak content

Plain pages that don't convert

No A+ content, no comparison charts, no video. Buyers leave with questions your page never answered.

Brand abuse

Hijackers and copycats on your listings

Brand Management Questions

01What does Amazon brand management include?

It covers everything that shapes how shoppers see your brand on Amazon: Brand Registry, product images, A+ and Premium A+ content, your Storefront, product videos, brand protection against hijackers and counterfeits, and a consistent brand voice across every listing.

02Do I need Brand Registry for these services?

A+ content, Storefronts, Sponsored Brands and most protection tools need Brand Registry. If you aren't enrolled yet, we guide you through trademark timing and enrollment first.

03Can you fix listings that look inconsistent across my catalog?

Yes. We create a brand style guide for Amazon (colors, fonts, image templates and tone of voice) and roll it out across every ASIN, so your catalog looks like one brand instead of many.

04How do you deal with hijackers and counterfeit sellers?

We monitor your listings, collect test-buy evidence when needed, file reports through Brand Registry, and use tools such as Transparency and Project Zero where you're eligible.

05How long until new brand content goes live?

Most image and A+ projects take 2 to 3 weeks from brief to upload. A full Storefront usually takes 3 to 4 weeks. Amazon's review of A+ content can add a few days.

06Do you work with brands outside the US marketplace?

Yes. We localize images, A+ content and Storefronts for UK, EU, Canada, Australia, Japan and other marketplaces, including translation by native speakers.
