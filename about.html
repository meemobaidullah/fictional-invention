Growth for your store. Protection for your account.

Meem started selling and managing marketplace stores in 2019. What began as one-person virtual assistant work grew into eCommeem, a full-service team that runs Amazon, Walmart, eBay, TikTok Shop and Temu stores for brands in the USA, Canada, the UK, the UAE, France and beyond.

His focus has always been two things at once: growing sales and keeping the account healthy, because growth means nothing if a store gets suspended. As a motivator and team builder, he has trained a team of 25 specialists who each own a part of the work, from listings and PPC to account health and brand design.
