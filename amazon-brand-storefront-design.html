Storefront Design Questions

01What is an Amazon Brand Store?

An Amazon Brand Store, or Storefront, is a free multi-page mini-site on Amazon for brands enrolled in Brand Registry. It shows your full product range, your brand story and your best sellers, with no competitor ads on the page.

02Do I need Brand Registry to get a Storefront?

Yes. Amazon only offers Stores to brands enrolled in Brand Registry. If you aren't enrolled yet, we help with that first.

03How many pages should my Storefront have?

Most brands need 3 to 8 pages: a homepage, a page per main category, a best sellers page and a brand story or about page. We plan the structure around how your customers shop.

04How long does a Storefront design take?

A typical Storefront takes 3 to 4 weeks from kickoff to going live, including two rounds of revisions and Amazon's review.

05Can you update my Storefront for sales events?

Yes. We create seasonal and event versions for Prime Day, Black Friday, holidays and new launches, and switch them on and off on schedule.

06How do I know if my Storefront is working?

We track visits, sales, units and traffic sources in the Stores insights dashboard and send a monthly report with what to change next.
