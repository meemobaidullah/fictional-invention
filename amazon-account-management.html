Account Management Questions

01What does Amazon account management include?

Daily store operations: listings and catalog, pricing and promotions, inventory and FBA shipments, Seller Support cases, account health monitoring, reimbursements and monthly reporting. Advertising can be added as part of the Growth Booster plan.

02Do you manage Vendor Central as well as Seller Central?

Yes. We manage both, including hybrid brands that sell 1P and 3P, and handle purchase orders, chargebacks and shortage claims for vendors.

03How much access do you need?

We use Seller Central's user permissions, so you decide exactly what we can see and change. You can remove access at any time.

04Which marketplaces can you manage?

All 17 Amazon marketplaces we support, from amazon.com and amazon.co.uk to amazon.ae and amazon.co.jp, plus Walmart, eBay, TikTok Shop and Temu.

05How quickly can you start?

Most stores are onboarded within a week of the free audit. Urgent account health issues are handled first.
