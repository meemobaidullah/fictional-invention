Listing Optimization Questions

01How long does listing optimization take?

Most listings are researched, rewritten and uploaded within 5 to 7 business days. Larger catalogs are scheduled in batches.

02Will changing my listing hurt my ranking?

Done carefully, no. We keep the keywords you already rank for and add the ones you are missing, then check indexing after upload.

03Do you write for marketplaces outside the US?

Yes. We localize copy for the UK, EU, Canada, UAE, Australia, Japan and more, using native-level writers.

04Can you fix suppressed listings?

Yes. We find the cause, whether attributes, category, images or restricted words, and fix it.

05Do you also optimize images?

Yes, through our brand team: main images, infographics, lifestyle shots and A+ content.
